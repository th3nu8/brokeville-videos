# Facts used (E002 nuclear power plant, block style)
- One uranium fuel pellet creates as much energy as one ton of coal. https://www.nei.org/fundamentals/nuclear-fuel
- A pellet is about the size of a thumbnail; pellets are stacked into fuel rods, bundled into assemblies in the core. https://energy.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/20260515_Nuclear-Fuel_OED-Fact-Sheet.pdf
- Fission releases heat and more neutrons that split other U-235 atoms (chain reaction). https://www.mining.com/web/the-power-of-a-uranium-pellet/
- Control rods absorb neutrons to control the rate of the reaction. https://courses.ems.psu.edu/earth104/node/1304
- PWRs keep the water around the fuel under pressure to prevent it from boiling. https://www.nei.org/news/2019/how-reactor-actually-works
- PWR: primary loop carries heat to the steam generator, which boils a secondary loop into steam; steam turns the turbine generator; exhausted steam is condensed and pumped back. https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/power/pwrs
- Cooling tower plumes are water vapor, not smoke. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooling_tower
