# Facts used (E001 nuclear bunker)
- Fallout shelters let occupants wait until fallout radioactivity decays to safer levels over a few weeks or months. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fallout_shelter
- Shielding is described in "halving thicknesses"; 10 halvings cut radiation to about 1/1024. https://hps.org/publicinformation/ate/q14414/
- Earth and concrete are common shielding; about a foot of earth makes an adequate shield. https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/policy/army/fm/3-3-1_2/Appb.htm
- Commercial bunkers often use reinforced concrete walls of about 50 cm. https://engineerfix.com/how-to-build-a-bunker-the-ultimate-step-by-step-guide/
- Common purpose-built shelter: steel-reinforced concrete vault, needing blast doors, air filtration, secondary exits and air-proofing; blast door edges counter-sunk into the frame. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blast_shelter
- NBC filtration: pre-filters, HEPA filters, activated carbon. https://engineerfix.com/how-to-build-a-bomb-shelter-for-maximum-protection/
- Shelters keep internal air pressure higher than outside so contaminated air can't get in. https://www.sciencefocus.com/future-technology/most-doomsday-preppers-have-it-wrong-this-is-actually-how-to-survive-the-apocalypse
- One main entrance plus an emergency escape hatch is common. https://hogonext.com/?p=454224
